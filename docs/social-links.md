# Social links

The home and contact pages share the same social buttons. Configure verified HTTPS profile URLs using SOCIAL_FACEBOOK, SOCIAL_TIKTOK, SOCIAL_INSTAGRAM, and SOCIAL_YOUTUBE in your hosting environment.

For YouTube, set SOCIAL_YOUTUBE to your official channel URL and redeploy. Without a URL, the YouTube button is shown as disabled with a “link coming soon” label, matching the other social buttons. Do not commit credentials or live environment files.
