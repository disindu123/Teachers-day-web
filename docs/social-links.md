# Official links

Home and Contact share social buttons. Configure verified HTTPS profile URLs using `SOCIAL_FACEBOOK`, `SOCIAL_TIKTOK`, `SOCIAL_INSTAGRAM`, `SOCIAL_YOUTUBE` and `SCHOOL_WEBSITE` in the host environment. Unknown profiles remain disabled until supplied.

YouTube defaults to [Sanghabo Pahasara](https://www.youtube.com/@SanghaboPahasara). Its icon uses the full red background on hover, keyboard focus and press. Email is [sangabopahasara@gmail.com](mailto:sangabopahasara@gmail.com).

Gallery albums use the **exact** Facebook album URL stored with each photograph, independent of the general Facebook social link. “View more on Facebook” opens that album. No URL is fabricated for an unlinked album.

Board members’ contact links are optional and editable by Admins only. Confirm that the members approve their public display.
